# 001 — launch-announcement — Design

> Example output of `specloop:design-closing`, closed from the requirements above.
> See `examples/README.md`.

## Approach

Write the landing page copy and email first, since the social posts quote lines from
both rather than the other way round. Route every asset through Legal in one batch
once all three are drafted, rather than one-at-a-time, so the "instant" claim gets one
consistent ruling instead of three separate ones that might disagree.

## Deliverables

- Landing page copy + layout brief for `/features/instant-export` (handed to the
  existing design contractor — no new tooling needed).
- Announcement email (subject line + body + one A/B subject variant).
- Three social posts: X thread (3 posts), LinkedIn post, 30-second video script for
  the short-form channel.
- Legal sign-off record (one thread, all three asset types attached).

## Sequencing

Landing page + email drafted first (in parallel) → social posts drafted from their
final copy → Legal review as one batch → publish in the order landing page → email →
social, so the CTA the email points to is already live.

## Open questions / deferred

- Whether the video script gets a real video produced in time for the event, or ships
  as a text post instead, is still open — deferred to whoever owns the event-week
  production calendar; asked again in that spec's own requirements if it becomes its
  own item.
