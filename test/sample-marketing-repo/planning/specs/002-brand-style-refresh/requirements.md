# 002 — brand-style-refresh

## What's being built

- A refresh of Loopwell's brand guidelines (voice, palette, typography), deferred
  until after the "Instant Export" launch.

## Who/what it serves

- Everyone producing customer-facing copy/design at Loopwell going forward.

## Hard constraints

- Cannot start until `001` publishes — the launch has to work within the *current*
  guidelines, not a moving target.

## Acceptance criteria

- A brand workshop has been run with stakeholders.
- An external design agency has been selected (if the workshop decides one is needed).
- Updated guidelines are ratified by the brand team.

## Out of scope

- Applying the refreshed guidelines to existing published material — that's a
  follow-up spec once the guidelines themselves are ratified.
