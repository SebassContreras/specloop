# Styles & preferences

## Brand

- Voice: direct, concrete claims backed by evidence. No hype adjectives
  ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless").
- Palette/typography: match the existing Loopwell brand site — no new palette for
  this launch. (Hard rule: don't introduce off-brand color.)

## Tone

- Confident but plain. Written for a technical buyer who's skeptical of marketing
  copy.
- Language: English.

## Accessibility

- Landing page must meet the existing site's accessibility baseline (already
  enforced by the CMS's templates) — no new target for this spec.

## Anti-preferences

- No countdown timers or urgency gimmicks ("only 3 days left!").
- No stock photography of people in suits shaking hands.

## Preference strength

- Voice/anti-preferences: hard rule.
- Palette/typography: hard rule (match existing brand, no deviation without a brand
  team sign-off, which isn't in scope for this spec).
