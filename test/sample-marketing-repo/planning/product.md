# Product

## What this is

Loopwell's public launch of its new "Instant Export" feature: the campaign that
announces it to existing customers and prospects. Project type: **marketing/content**.

## Who uses it

- Existing customers (~40k email subscribers) who'll see the announcement and the new
  feature in-product.
- Prospects who land on the feature page from organic search or social.

## Out of scope

- Paid ad spend for this launch — organic channels only for the first phase.
- Localization — English-only until the feature's traction is known.

## MVP / first phase

Get the "Instant Export" launch announcement out the door for the "Loopwell 3.0"
event week: landing page, announcement email, social posts. Everything else (brand
refresh, future campaigns) comes later.

## Done when

The launch week has passed, the page/email/posts are all live, and Legal has signed
off on every asset that published.

## Hard constraints

- Must publish during the existing "Loopwell 3.0" event week — fixed date, not
  movable.
- Every asset needs Legal sign-off before publishing (speed/performance claims).

## Stakeholders

- Legal (mandatory sign-off).
- The design contractor (landing page layout).

## Automatability

Drafting copy is agent work. Anything that requires the actual live-publish action
(sending the email, publishing the page/posts), a Legal approval, or the design
contractor's involvement stays with a human.
